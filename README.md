# Volotome Privacy Policy

*Effective 10 October 2026*

Volotome is an audiobook player for iPhone and Apple Watch, made by me. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Volotome does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. Everything stays on your devices.

## Your audiobooks

The audiobooks you add, their covers and your listening positions are stored only on your devices, inside Volotome. They are not included in iCloud backups.

When you choose a cover from your photos, Volotome receives only the photo you pick. It cannot see the rest of your library.

## Listening statistics

Volotome can count how long and when you listen so the Statistics tab can show fun facts. This information is stored only on your devices and is never sent to me or anyone else.

You can turn statistics off in Settings at any time, which permanently deletes them. If you export your listening data, the file goes only where you choose to save or send it.

## Apple Watch

If you use Volotome on Apple Watch, your library, listening positions, covers, audiobooks you choose to store on the watch, and listening statistics are sent directly between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch by iOS and watchOS. None of it passes through any server of mine.

## Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may give me anonymous crash reports and usage figures. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and can be switched off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

## If you email me

If you contact me, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply to you, never share it, and delete it once it's no longer needed.

## Children

Volotome does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
